Process

Work Process

Every project starts with understanding the audience and business goal. From there I map the experience, design the interface, build the product, and refine the details before launch.

1. Research

Understand the problem, users, competitors, and project goals before making design decisions.

2. Analyze

Turn research into clear priorities, user flows, content structure, and measurable requirements.

3. Design

Create polished layouts, reusable components, responsive states, and interaction details.

4. Launch

Build, test, deploy, and improve the experience using real feedback after release.
